Privacy

Effective October 14, 2026

This notice covers AniRec at anirec.lukasschulmeyer.com, an independent, noncommercial project by Lukas Schulmeyer.

What this website collects

AniRec receives the title searches and selected anime IDs you submit so it can find titles and recommendations. The server sends search text and title IDs to the official MyAnimeList API. It temporarily caches public responses and reuses requests to reduce waiting and repeated provider calls.

AniRec has no accounts or MyAnimeList account connection. It does not request your watch history or personal ratings, maintain an account-based search history, or use advertising trackers or application analytics. It does not set application cookies. Your English or Romaji title-display preference is saved in this browser's local storage so it is remembered on your next visit; clearing site data removes it. The open page keeps temporary title information in memory.

Selected titles in links

Your selected anime IDs and format filter appear in result-page URLs so you can refresh or share the same selection. These URLs may remain in browser history and ordinary hosting request records. Anyone you share a results link with can see the selected titles.

Hosting and ordinary requests

The website is hosted by Vercel. Visiting it sends ordinary request information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested URL, and request time, to the hosting provider. Vercel may process this information to deliver and secure the website under its own policies. Read the Vercel privacy policy for details.

Fonts and application assets are served from this website. Anime posters load from MyAnimeList's image servers, which receive ordinary image-request information such as your IP address. Requests use a no-referrer policy so your results URL is not sent as a referrer. Read MyAnimeList's privacy policy for its practices.

External links

Links to MyAnimeList, Vercel, and Lukas’s portfolio lead to separate websites. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them. AniRec does not receive your account information from those websites.

Future features

This notice will be updated before new features change how the application handles personal information.

Questions

For questions about this project or this notice, use the contact options on Lukas Schulmeyer’s portfolio.
