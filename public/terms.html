About AniRec

AniRec is an independent, noncommercial anime discovery project by Lukas Schulmeyer. Search for anime, select favorites, and explore community recommendations. No account or account connection is required.

Recommendations and availability

Results combine the community connections returned by MyAnimeList for your selected titles. The returned lists may omit other connections. Missing information stays unknown, and recommendations are suggestions rather than a prediction of enjoyment. Metadata, provider access, features, and availability can change.

Community sources and affiliation

The recommendation experience uses connections made by anime fans on MyAnimeList. AniRec is not affiliated with or endorsed by MyAnimeList. Anime artwork, source material, and referenced names belong to their respective owners. Links to source title pages are provided in the details.
